Vercel: Geist Pixel (Feb 2026)

A bitmap face with five styles (Square, Grid, Circle, Triangle, Line), built as an extension of a type system rather than a novelty font.

→ #03 Spark uses Circle-style dots; the wordmarks use Geist Pixel.

vercel.com/blog

Mistral AI's pixel "M"

Cited as the clearest AI brand using a pixelated, striped mark that nods to early computing, in contrast to OpenAI's minimalism and Gemini's spark.

→ #10 CRT (scanline stripes), #03 Spark (an AI spark, but pixel-built).

ebaqdesign.com

Pixel and Y2K logo comeback

Several 2026 trend write-ups say pixel logos are back. They read as gaming, technology and the 90s, and they stand out in crowded digital spaces.

→ #05 Marquee, #09 Extrude, #13 Glitch.

brandcrowd.com · dubaimonsters.com · dev.to

Adaptive identity systems

Inkbot Design's view of 2026: logos become systems with responsive variants and pixel-sharp precision at small sizes, and they can move.

→ Every card is tested at 12/14/18/24/28px; several marks animate (cursor, orb, voice, eye).

inkbotdesign.com

Dithering on the web (Codrops, CodePen, Awwwards)

Bayer-matrix ordered dithering is back as a deliberate texture, for example the 4×4 Bayer pixel transitions in Codrops portfolios and the Awwwards "Pixel Effect" element.

→ #08 Dither Orb is a real 4×4 Bayer dither, drawn live.

codrops · codepen · awwwards

Dribbble pixel lettermarks

Pixel letter logos by Pixasquare and the "Eight-Bit Inspiration" collection: single-letter marks with one accent pixel and a strong silhouette.

→ #01 Keystone D, #14 dn ligature.
