Donna · brand · round 4

Lady of the house.

"Donna" comes from the Latin domina, "mistress of the house", from domus, house. That is exactly the job of an operational assistant: she holds the keys, signs things off, writes on your behalf and keeps the day running. These 12 marks are built from that idea and from the studio work below. Each is a simple geometric symbol drawn to work as an app icon first. Use Construction to see the grid each one is drawn on, and Finish to try them in chrome, copper or one colour.
