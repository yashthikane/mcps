Donna: Soft Glass variations

Five new takes on the Soft Glass direction, shown on the same Donna screen. The original comes first for reference and is unchanged, both here and in ui-demos.html. The new variants add slowly drifting light behind the glass. Use the Ambient light button to pause it.

Liquid Glass

The most transparent option. Nearly clear panels with gradient edges, a highlight along the top of each surface, and a tinted blue accent.
